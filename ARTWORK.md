# Artwork in 1.2.1

Eight small existing images from Wikimedia Commons are bundled offline and shared across stations by genre. These are illustrative covers, not official station logos or covers of the episode currently playing. No generated image assets are included.

The station list, full-screen player and media metadata use this artwork. Images retain their proportions and the full cover is visible. Tap Artwork credits in the player for the image title, creator, license and source page.

The authoritative per-image source and license inventory is `app/src/main/assets/artwork/credits.json`. The microphone photograph is CC0; the seven historical illustrations are marked Public domain by Wikimedia Commons. Public-domain status can differ by jurisdiction; retain the source records when distributing elsewhere.

The bundled thumbnails total approximately 1 MB. Radio/variety/drama use the microphone photograph; adventure, western, science fiction/horror, crime, mystery/suspense, comedy and seasonal channels use the corresponding historical illustration. Multiple stations share an image. This release does not introduce additional radio stations or change stream URLs.
