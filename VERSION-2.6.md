# OTR Dial 2.6 preview

This iteration layers a checked source expansion on top of the existing 2.5 catalogue:

- 16 newly verified English-language live streams, including Australian Digital Radio Network channels, 1920s Radio Network, BygoneRadio, Radio Mystery Theater USA, Radio OnceMore and Stupid Potato series streams.
- 52 publisher RSS feeds from Great Detectives, Great Adventurers, Retro Radio Podcast, CBSRMT, OTRWesterns, Old Time Retro Radio and Theater of the Mind.
- 17 selected Internet Archive OTRR collections, loaded only when the listener opens a source.
- Three additional YouTube source links; YouTube remains link-only and is not downloaded or played in the background by OTR Dial.
- The source and station assets are layered as optional 2.6 files so the original catalogue remains recoverable and missing optional assets do not break startup.

The research package in `../research-2.6` records source pages, sample checks and the larger inventory. Availability and rights remain controlled by the original publishers and archives.
